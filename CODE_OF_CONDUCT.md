# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in this project. We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

We expect all contributors, maintainers, users, and participants to communicate respectfully and professionally.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information about others without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

Maintainers may take appropriate and reasonable action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions related to this project, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

Reports should include enough information for the maintainers to understand what happened and, where possible, provide relevant evidence.

Maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate action.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take one or more of the following actions depending on the situation:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction from participating in project activities.
4. Permanent restriction from participating in project activities.

The response will depend on the seriousness and circumstances of the violation.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by community standards and open-source Code of Conduct practices, including the Contributor Covenant.
